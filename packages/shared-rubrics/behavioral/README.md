# Behavioral Interview Guidelines (STAR Framework)

## STAR Method Overview
All behavioral answers should follow the STAR framework:
- **S**ituation — Context and background
- **T**ask — Your specific responsibility
- **A**ction — What you did and why
- **R**esult — Quantifiable outcome and lessons learned

---

## Common Behavioral Categories

### Leadership & Influence
1. Tell me about a time you led a project with ambiguous requirements.
2. Describe a situation where you had to influence without authority.
3. Give an example of mentoring a junior team member.

### Conflict Resolution
1. Tell me about a disagreement with a coworker. How did you resolve it?
2. Describe a time you received critical feedback. What did you do?
3. How did you handle a situation where your manager disagreed with your approach?

### Problem Solving Under Pressure
1. Describe a production incident you resolved under time pressure.
2. Tell me about a time you had to make a decision with incomplete information.
3. Give an example of a project that didn't go as planned. What happened?

### Customer Focus & Impact
1. Describe a feature you shipped that significantly impacted users.
2. Tell me about a time you advocated for the customer against team consensus.
3. Give an example of using data to drive a product decision.

---

## Evaluation Rubric (Behavioral)

| Dimension | 1 (Poor) | 3 (Average) | 5 (Excellent) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Structure | No STAR format | Partial STAR | Clear STAR throughout |
| Specificity | Vague, hypothetical | Some concrete details | Specific, quantified outcomes |
| Self-Awareness | No reflection | Basic lessons learned | Deep insights & growth mindset |
| Relevance | Off-topic | Somewhat related | Directly addresses the question |
| Communication | Rambling, unfocused | Adequate clarity | Concise, compelling narrative |

---

## Interviewer Tips
- Limit each answer to 3–4 minutes maximum
- Ask ONE follow-up per answer for depth
- Score independently before discussion
- Note specific examples of STAR adherence or gaps
